Methodology

How DealScope evaluates a transaction

Strategic fit

Tests whether the target strengthens the buyer’s long-term priorities, capabilities, customers, or competitive position.

Cultural fit

Examines operating style, decision-making, incentives, talent norms, and the likelihood of integration friction.

Geographic fit

Considers regulatory jurisdictions, distance, language, market familiarity, and cross-border operating complexity.

Valuation comfort

Assesses premium, financing, debt burden, synergy dependence, and the margin for execution error.

Shareholder impact

Separates immediate target-holder gains from longer-term acquirer returns and potential dilution.

Evidence confidence

Labels automated detections separately and avoids presenting inferred facts as verified transaction terms.
