Sources and research standards

The dashboard uses reputable business publications for discovery and encourages direct verification of transaction terms.

Editorial standards

How sources are handled

01

Primary reporting first

Transaction values, status, and stated intent should be verified against original reporting or company announcements.

02

Automation is labeled

RSS-detected stories are marked “News detected” until researched and promoted into the curated dataset.

03

Analysis is separated

Strategic interpretation and scoring are clearly distinct from verified deal facts.

Publication access and reuse are subject to each outlet’s terms. DealScope links to source reporting rather than reproducing full articles.
